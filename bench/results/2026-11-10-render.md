# Render benchmark (2026-11-10)

- **GitInside:** `f99a9991d6e0aa05b992d19a315ffa69bb9ad1c0`
- **Graphs:** a synthetic graph (10 services broken down into routes or pages, `bench/src/renderGraphs.ts`) and calcom (https://github.com/calcom/cal.com at `0f19cc46ef6b6426ee398c670575b3c96be7af9e`), each opened to the level closest to 500, 1,000, and 2,000 visible nodes without passing it, under the explorer's default filters.
- **Method:** the explorer in a production build (`bench/render/`), Chromium 156.0.8078.4 (headless), 1440×900, dark theme. After the first layout, the camera is zoomed out to the minimum (25%) and moved to the spot with the most boxes drawn (a scan, a canvas at a time), then 2 s left idle, then 10 s of Playwright panning and zooming (wheel pan, Ctrl+wheel zoom in, a drag, Ctrl+wheel zoom out, wheel pan back). fps comes from `requestAnimationFrame` timestamps: the median, and p5 (the 95th-percentile frame time) (EVALUATION.md §2).
- **Machine:** dev machine: 13th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i7-13700H (20 logical cores), 15.6 GiB RAM, win32 10.0.26300, Node v22.13.0

## Result

- **synthetic, 2,000 visible nodes:** 59.9 fps median, 59.5 fps p5: meets the 60fps median target.
- **calcom, 2,000 visible nodes:** 59.9 fps median, 59.5 fps p5: meets the 60fps median target.

A run meets the target when its median fps rounds to 60 or more; requestAnimationFrame never runs faster than the display, so the idle column is the ceiling.

## Observations (hand-written, not generated)

- **The launch viewer (M9.2d), run at `f99a999`** on a clean tree. The 2026-10-31 report was taken with uncommitted changes. cal.com's graph comes from the launch analyzer (0.3.0).
- **Every case runs at the display's ceiling:** 59.9 fps median, 59.5 fps p5, and the idle rate is 59.9 too (2026-10-31: the same, except a 59.9 p5 for cal.com at 500 and synthetic at 2,000). So this measures "60 fps met". It doesn't measure headroom, and it can't rank the cases.
- **What was on screen:** at 2,000 visible nodes the canvas drew 102 cal.com boxes at the start and 209 synthetic ones, with 103–114 and 196–211 in the DOM during the run. The explorer draws only what's in view, which is why fps doesn't fall as the visible count grows. cal.com at 1,000 nodes dropped to 11 boxes at one point, while zoomed in.
- **Long frames:** 0–3 frames per case took over 50 ms, of 574–599. The worst frame was 50–100 ms.
- **Time to first drawn layout:** 0.9–2.5 s up to 1,000 nodes, 1.4 s for the synthetic 2,000, and 11.0 s for cal.com's 2,000 (2026-10-31: 9.8 s). Analysis runs on this laptop were about 3× slower than the machine can be this session (`2026-11-10-incremental-calcom-commits.md`). Whether that held during this run wasn't measured, so the 11.0 s is this session's number, not a regression measurement.
